Chapter 1 Transmigrated? Into a Book?
"I'll take a shower first."
A man's deep, magnetic voice came from behind her, sending a shiver down Song Yunfei's spine.
She snapped her eyes open, and what met her gaze was a mottled, old wall, with peeling and cracked plaster in some places, covered in colorful graffiti and writings done by someone with markers.
Song Yunfei was a bit dazed. Wasn't she just in a taxi? Where had she ended up now?
Before she could figure it out, a fierce hunger panged in her stomach, and her whole head felt dizzy.
Catching the aroma of food in the air, Song Yunfei forced herself to get up.
Only then did she realize that she was lying on a bed.
It was a fairly large single room, with the dining table and the bed all in one space, and the bathroom diagonally across from them.
Not far behind her, the man was taking off his coat.
The man was broad-shouldered and long-legged, with deep-set eyes and chiseled features, giving off an aloof demeanor.
It was just that the shit-yellow delivery uniform on him clashed with his aura.
Song Yunfei blinked and stared at the man for a long while.
The man seemed to notice her gaze, took off the delivery uniform, draped it over the chair, and glanced up at her.
"Eat your meal first. After you finish showering... I'll come."
His face was expressionless. After saying this, he went to the bathroom.
Song Yunfei was still in a daze, planning to fill her stomach first before worrying about anything else.
However, just as she was about to get out of bed, a buzzing sound rang in her head, and a surge of memories that did not belong to her flooded in.
She froze on the spot.
Tr... Transmigrated into a book?
She remembered! She remembered everything!
I was in a taxi heading to the airport, killing time by reading a novel on my phone, when a sports car ran a red light and smashed head-on into us. Then I was sucked into the very novel I had been reading.
